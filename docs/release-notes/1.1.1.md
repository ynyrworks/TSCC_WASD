# TSCC_WASD 1.1.1

## 繁體中文

修正搖桿校準可能存下錯誤中心點的問題。

**如果你用 1.1.0 校準過，而且搖桿放開後還會自己飄移：** 更新後打開「校準搖桿」重新校準一次，或按「清除校準」改回手把原廠校準。

- 修正：「放開搖桿」這一步可能存下離實際位置很遠的中心點，放開的搖桿會一直被當成往某個方向推（在藍牙連線的 Switch Pro 上發現）。原因是手把沒有送新資料時，同一筆舊資料會被重複計算，也沒有檢查資料是否穩定、中心點是否合理。現在只採用手把真的送來的新資料；搖桿在動或中心點離正中間太遠時，會請你放開搖桿重來；收不到資料時會提示手把沒有送資料。

從 1.1.0 或 1.0.0 在「關於與說明」按「檢查更新」即可，程式會先核對 SHA-256 才安裝。手動下載請用 `.sha256` 檔核對 `TSCC_WASD-1.1.1-win-x64.zip`。本版沒有數位簽章。

---

## English

Fixes stick calibration saving a wrong center.

**If you calibrated with 1.1.0 and a released stick drifts on its own:** after updating, open *Calibrate sticks* and calibrate again, or click *Clear calibration* to go back to the controller's factory calibration.

- Fixed: the "release the sticks" step could save a center far from the real one, so a released stick kept reading as pushed (found on a Switch Pro over Bluetooth). When the controller sent nothing new, the last reading was counted again, and neither steadiness nor a plausible center was checked. The wizard now uses only new readings, starts the step again if the sticks move or the center is far from the middle, and says when the controller is not sending data.

Update from 1.1.0 or 1.0.0 with *Check for updates* on About & help; the app checks the SHA-256 before installing. For a manual download, check `TSCC_WASD-1.1.1-win-x64.zip` against its `.sha256` file. This release is not code-signed.
